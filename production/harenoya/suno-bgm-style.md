Instrumental Japanese summer romance visual novel BGM, 98 BPM. Breezy acoustic pop with a gentle bossa nova touch, set in a welcoming seaside beach house on a sunny afternoon. Warm nylon-string guitar, soft felt piano, airy Rhodes, rounded bass, delicate shaker and brushed drums, with occasional glockenspiel accents like sunlight on water. A simple, memorable melodic motif, tender major-seventh and add9 harmonies, playful everyday warmth and a subtle blush of romance. No vocals, no humming, no dramatic drops, no heavy percussion.
